Common Questions

FAQ

What types of projects are the best fit for Tomorrow Lab?

We work best with hardware product projects that have real business intent — from a working prototype to a manufacturable product. We serve startups, corporate innovation teams, and advertising agencies building physical concepts. The ideal project has concept clarity, budget, and a team committed to taking it to market.

Can you work with internal corporate innovation teams?

Yes. We regularly work alongside corporate R&D, innovation, and product teams as a drop-in hardware expertise partner — providing enhancement, training, expansion, and gap-filling for existing in-house teams without disrupting your internal structure.

Do you partner with advertising and creative agencies?

Absolutely. We've built wearable experiences for National Geographic with McCann NY, connected brand activations with BBDO, and holiday product experiences with USPS. We understand agency timelines, stakeholder dynamics, and the standard of quality your client expects.

What happens during the discovery/prototype phase?

Our 2-week Discovery Intensive and 6–8 week Holistic Innovation Sprint cover concept definition, feasibility review, design research, technology strategy, industrial design exploration, and a prototype or rendering output. You'll leave with a clear product direction, documented risks, and a roadmap for next steps.

Do we own the IP from our engagement?

Yes. When a project is complete, you own the work. Our goal is to create IP that is relevant and valuable to your company. We can also assist with patent strategy and documentation as part of the engagement.

How quickly can a project begin?

Depending on current availability, we can typically begin within 2–4 weeks of a signed agreement. Our calendar books in advance — reach out early. Book a discovery call and we'll align on a start date that works for your timeline.
